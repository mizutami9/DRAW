# SE audit — 2026-08-25

## Scope

- All 45 saved stages (`1-1` through `15-3`) were found and parsed.
- Shared player actions were checked: movement, jump/landing, carrying/throwing,
  species actions, damage, death, respawn and emotes.
- Shared stage objects were checked: goals, collectibles, switches, linked doors,
  jump pads, speed boosts, bombs, dynamite, beams, guns, cannons, missiles,
  enemies, breakable floors/walls/crates and keys.
- Runtime challenge controllers were checked, including survival, pillar, rope,
  boss, ricochet, coin, circuit, escort, tower-defense and final-boss stages.

## Existing coverage retained

- UI, DRAW and runtime editor feedback
- Player footsteps, jump, landing, hit, death and respawn
- Human lift/throw, cat movement/claws, bird flap/glide, turtle movement and
  slime attachment
- Bomb/dynamite fuse, countdown and explosion
- Enemy charge/shoot/jump/defeat/shell collision
- Beam, cannon, emote and crumbling-floor feedback

## Missing coverage added

- Common flow: countdown tick, START, clear, failure and goal arrival
- Collection: coin and generic collectible pickup
- Stage mechanisms: switches, opening/closing doors, jump pads, speed boosts,
  key unlocks, pillar warnings/impacts and wooden crate impacts/breaks
- Combat: gun shots, missile launch/impact, projectile ricochet and boss charge
- Boss stages 4-3, 15-1, 15-2 and 15-3: attack warning, dash, beam charge,
  suction, homing-missile launch/impact, collapsing-floor warning/impact and
  phase countdown feedback
- Stage 13-3: partial circuit connection and completed-circuit feedback
- Turtle: entering and leaving the shell

All added clips are resolved through `SfxId` and `SfxCatalog`; stage code does not
contain direct asset paths. `GameSfx` keeps the existing SE master-volume setting,
pooling, spatial playback and per-sound cooldown behavior.

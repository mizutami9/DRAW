All files in this directory are taken from the Kenney packs listed below and
are licensed under Creative Commons CC0 1.0. Attribution is not required.
The source URLs and original filenames are retained here for auditing.

- UI Audio: https://kenney.nl/assets/ui-audio
- Impact Sounds: https://kenney.nl/assets/impact-sounds
- RPG Audio: https://kenney.nl/assets/rpg-audio
- Digital Audio: https://kenney.nl/assets/digital-audio
- Music Jingles: https://kenney.nl/assets/music-jingles (downloaded as a source pack; no file from it is currently assigned)
- CC0 1.0: https://creativecommons.org/publicdomain/zero/1.0/
- Kenney licensing FAQ: https://kenney.nl/support

Do not remove this file when replacing or redistributing the audio directory.
The DRAW sounds are temporary approximations using cloth/book foley and should
eventually be replaced with recordings of pencil, crayon and eraser on paper.
